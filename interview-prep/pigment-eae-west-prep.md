**The truth:** You have never sold business planning, FP&A, or EPM software. Your background is CLM, IT automation, and device management.

**Bridge language:** "I haven't sold EPM software specifically, but I've spent the last four years selling platforms that fundamentally change how business leaders make decisions — which is exactly what Pigment does. At Agiloft, I sold to General Counsel and CFOs who needed to understand how contract intelligence would change their operational model. The discovery motion, the business case, the C-suite alignment — that's the same selling motion. The domain knowledge around FP&A is a 30-day ramp; the enterprise selling discipline is an eight-year asset."

**Supporting proof:** Agiloft (multi-stakeholder with finance/legal/procurement, business case/ROI selling), Aisera (operational transformation pitch to Finance/Operations leadership).

**Supporting proof:** ROI model creation at Agiloft, executive sponsorship and business case development, Aisera ARR growth tied to operations/finance outcomes.

## Questions to Ask the Recruiter

These signal preparation, commercial thinking, and genuine interest. Pick 3–4.

1. **Territory definition:** "Can you help me understand how the West territory is structured — is this a named account model, geographic, or segment-based? And how much of the pipeline would I be inheriting vs. building from scratch?"

2. **Stage and ramp:** "Where does Pigment stand in terms of West Coast brand recognition and customer presence? I want to understand whether I'd be walking into a market that knows Pigment or one where I'd be doing a lot of education-first selling."

3. **The SC/pre-sales motion:** "How does the partnership between Account Executives and Solution Consultants work at Pigment? I like to bring my SC in early — is that how the team typically operates?"

4. **The CS handoff:** "One thing I care about is ensuring customers are set up to actually realize value post-close — that drives expansion and referrals. What does the AE-to-CSM handoff look like here?"

5. **Hiring timeline:** "What does your ideal timeline look like from here through an offer? And what are the key things the Hiring Manager will be evaluating in Round 2?"

6. **What makes a top performer:** "What separates AEs who hit President's Club at Pigment from those who struggle? What does the top performer do differently?"
