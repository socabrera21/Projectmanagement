## ORIGINAL JOB DESCRIPTION (Verbatim)

### What You'll Do

- Lead the full sales-cycle, from initial interest to deal close and coordinate collaboration cross-functionally with customer success, technical teams, etc.
- Create detailed business plans designed to attain predetermined goals and quotas
- Build relationships with executives at all levels and in all industries, in order to close deals
- Consult with prospects and existing customers regarding business objectives and requirements, and build long-lasting partnerships (5000+ employees scale-ups to enterprise)
- Model a wide range of use cases in which Pigment can drive business transformation across different industries
- Provide professional after-sales support with our CSM team to maximize customer loyalty
- Negotiate agreements and keep records of sales and data in Salesforce
- Think critically about sales insights and data and experiment with new growth tactics

### Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's Degree or equivalent practical experience
- 6+ years of experience in a quota-carrying Sales role within Tech or Financial Services
- Proven track record of success (President's Club, Chairman's Club)
- Proven attitude of going above and beyond expectations

### Preferred Qualifications

- Master's Degree, other advanced degree or equivalent practical experience
- Sales experience within SaaS B2B or similar financial product primarily targeting the US/North American market
- Experience in finance or business planning and have a personal passion for tech & tech products
- Deep track record of creating significant revenue impact and cultivating relationships for your organization

## 5. THE HIDDEN REQUIREMENTS

What Pigment is actually hiring for, beyond the JD language:

**1. Someone who can sell against entrenched incumbents without flinching.**
Pigment competes against Anaplan (dominant), Workday Adaptive, OneStream, SAP, and Oracle. The AE they hire needs to be comfortable walking into accounts where a $2M Anaplan contract just renewed, finding the dissatisfied champion, and building a displacement motion. The JD language "business transformation" is code for "help us unseat legacy tools."

**2. Someone with executive presence and gravitas at the CFO/VP Finance level.**
EPM/FP&A is a CFO-adjacent sale. The economic buyer is often the CFO, VP Finance, or Head of FP&A. Pigment needs an AE who can hold a peer-level conversation with a finance executive — not just sell features, but speak the language of planning cycles, headcount variance, and scenario modeling. The JD says "executives at all levels" but the implied buyer is finance leadership.

**3. Someone who can run complex, multi-stakeholder enterprise deals independently.**
The JD says "coordinate cross-functionally" but what they mean is: can you manage a 6–12 month deal with 4–8 stakeholders across Finance, IT, and the business without constant management oversight? Pigment is growing fast — they need an AE who doesn't need their hand held through enterprise complexity.

**4. A territory builder, not just a territory harvester.**
"Greenfield" is implied. The West territory likely has some existing accounts but is largely uncultivated. They want someone who can create demand, not just respond to inbound. The JD's language about "experimenting with new growth tactics" confirms this — they're looking for a builder.

**5. Someone who can translate product capability into business outcomes across industries.**
EPM is not a single-use-case tool — Pigment models headcount for a tech company, revenue planning for a consumer brand, and cost allocation for a manufacturing firm. The AE needs to be able to adapt the value story quickly. The JD phrase "model a wide range of use cases" is a real requirement, not filler.

**6. A candidate with pattern-matching speed in enterprise discovery.**
Pigment likely has a well-defined discovery process. They want an AE who can quickly qualify whether an account is a real opportunity — because the sales cycle is long and capacity is finite. MEDDPICC or equivalent is the implied framework.

**7. Cultural fit: velocity, ambition, low ego, high accountability.**
Pigment is a scaling Series C/D company. They're not IBM — they want someone who works fast, has high ownership mentality, and does not hide behind process. The OTE range ($280K–$320K) signals they want a top performer, not an order-taker.
