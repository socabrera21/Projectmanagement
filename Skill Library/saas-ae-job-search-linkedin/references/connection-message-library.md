# Connection Message Library

A collection of proven LinkedIn connection request templates for different scenarios in your SaaS AE job search. All messages are under 300 characters and follow the "Specific + Curious" framework.

## General Interest (No Specific Role)

### Template 1: Company Growth Signal
> Hi [Name], I noticed [Company] recently [raised Series B/launched new product/expanded into new market]. Impressive growth in the [industry] space. I'm exploring AE opportunities and would love to connect.

### Template 2: Mutual Connection
> Hi [Name], I'm connected with [Mutual Name] and saw your work at [Company]. I'm exploring SaaS sales roles in [vertical]—would love to connect and follow your insights.

### Template 3: Content Engagement
> Hi [Name], your recent post about [specific topic] resonated with me. I'm an AE in the [industry] space and would love to connect and stay engaged with your content.

### Template 4: Industry Focus
> Hi [Name], I'm targeting AE roles in [fintech/healthtech/etc.] SaaS and came across [Company]'s approach to [specific problem]. Your team's work is impressive—would love to connect.

## After Applying to a Role

### Template 5: Applied + Specific Interest
> Hi [Name], I applied for the AE role and was impressed by [Company]'s [specific product feature/market approach]. I have [X years] in [relevant area] and would love to connect.

### Template 6: Applied + Value Alignment
> Hi [Name], just applied for the AE position. [Company]'s focus on [customer success/product-led growth/etc.] aligns with my approach to sales. Excited to connect and learn more.

### Template 7: Applied + Recent News
> Hi [Name], applied for the AE role after seeing [Company]'s recent [announcement/milestone]. I'm excited about the [market/product] and would love to connect.

## Follow-Up Messages (After Acceptance)

These are sent within 48 hours of connection acceptance.

### Follow-Up 1: Applied Role
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! I applied for the AE role and am excited about [Company]'s work in [space]. I'd love to chat if there's a fit—either way, great to be connected.

### Follow-Up 2: General Interest
> Thanks for connecting! I'm impressed by [Company]'s approach to [specific area]. I'm exploring AE opportunities and would love to stay in touch as your team grows.

### Follow-Up 3: Offer Value
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I recently read [article/report] on [relevant topic] and thought of [Company]. Happy to share if it's useful. Excited to follow your work.

### Follow-Up 4: Ask a Question
> Thanks for connecting! I'm curious about [Company]'s approach to [specific strategy/market]. Would love to hear your perspective if you have a moment.

## Tips for Customization

1. **Replace placeholders:** Always fill in [Company], [Name], [specific detail]
2. **Research first:** Spend 2-3 minutes on their profile to find something specific
3. **Be concise:** LinkedIn cuts off at 300 characters—test your message length
4. **Sound human:** Avoid corporate jargon; write like you'd talk
5. **No typos:** One typo can tank your credibility—proofread

## What to Avoid

- ❌ "I'd like to add you to my professional network" (generic default)
- ❌ "I'm reaching out because I'm looking for a job" (too direct)
- ❌ "I'm the perfect fit for this role" (presumptuous)
- ❌ "Can you refer me?" (immediate ask)
- ❌ "Let me know if you have 15 minutes to chat" (too much, too soon)

## Response Rate Benchmarks

- **Generic message:** 10-15% acceptance
- **Personalized message:** 40-50% acceptance
- **Mutual connection mention:** 50-60% acceptance
- **Follow-up response rate:** 15-25% of accepted connections

If your rates are below these, increase personalization and specificity.
