# SaaS AE Job Search on LinkedIn

## Overview

This skill guides you through a strategic LinkedIn job search workflow for Account Executive roles in SaaS companies. The approach combines job discovery, company research, and relationship-building through low-pressure networking with hiring managers and recruiters. The goal is visibility and relationship-building, not immediate asks.

## Core Workflow

### 1. Job Search Strategy

**Search for roles using:**
- Job titles: "Account Executive," "Sales Account Executive," "Enterprise AE," "Strategic Account Executive"
- Keywords: "SaaS," "B2B," "software sales," "cloud," "enterprise software"
- Filters: Remote options, experience level, date posted (prioritize last 7-14 days for fresh opportunities)
- Boolean operators: `("Account Executive" OR "Sales Executive") AND (SaaS OR "software sales") AND remote`

**Create job alerts:**
- Set up 2-3 targeted alerts with different keyword combinations
- Daily or weekly frequency depending on urgency
- Review alerts within 24-48 hours of posting for best response rates

**Prioritize opportunities:**
- Under 10 applicants (higher visibility)
- Companies with strong growth signals (recent funding, hiring sprees, product launches)
- Roles matching your experience level and quota history
- Company culture and values alignment

### 2. Company Research

For each target company:
- **Product/market fit:** Understand what they sell and who they sell to
- **Recent news:** Funding rounds, product launches, leadership changes
- **Growth indicators:** Employee count trends, job posting volume, LinkedIn engagement
- **Sales team structure:** Size of sales org, recent AE hires, team expansion signals

This context makes your outreach credible and helps you decide if the company is worth pursuing.

### 3. Identify Key Contacts

**Hiring managers:**
- Search: `[Company Name] ("Sales Manager" OR "Sales Director" OR "VP Sales" OR "Head of Sales")`
- Filter by current employees at the company
- Prioritize those who posted the role or are tagged in job announcements

**Recruiters:**
- Search: `[Company Name] (Recruiter OR "Talent Acquisition" OR "Hiring Manager")`
- Internal recruiters are better than agency recruiters for direct relationships
- Look for recruiters who specialize in sales or GTM roles

**2nd-degree connections:**
- Mutual connections can increase acceptance rates by 30-40%
- Check if you share connections with target contacts
- Consider asking for a warm intro if the mutual connection is strong

### 4. Connection Request Strategy

**Timing:**
- Send requests Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 3 PM in their time zone
- After applying to a role: wait 24-48 hours, then connect
- Before applying: connect first if you want to gather intel

**Tone:**
- Low-pressure and genuine
- Express interest in the company, not just the job
- Show you've done research (mention a specific project, product, or company initiative)
- No immediate asks—focus on building a relationship

**Message structure (under 300 characters):**
1. **Specific reference:** Mention something real about their work, company, or content
2. **Genuine interest:** Why you're reaching out (not just "I want a job")
3. **Optional:** Brief mention of role if you've applied, but don't lead with it

**Examples:**

*To a hiring manager (after applying):*
> Hi [Name], I recently applied for the AE role and was impressed by [Company]'s approach to [specific product/market]. I'd love to connect and follow your work in the [industry] space.

*To a hiring manager (general interest):*
> Hi [Name], I'm exploring opportunities in [industry] SaaS and noticed [Company]'s recent [product launch/funding/initiative]. Your team's work is impressive—would love to connect and stay in touch.

*To a recruiter:*
> Hi [Name], I saw you're recruiting for sales roles at [Company]. I'm a SaaS AE with [X years] in [specific area] and would love to connect about current or future opportunities.

*With a mutual connection:*
> Hi [Name], I'm connected with [Mutual Connection] and noticed your work at [Company]. I'm exploring AE roles in [industry]—would love to connect and learn more about your team.

**What NOT to say:**
- ❌ "I'd like to add you to my professional network" (generic)
- ❌ "I'm the perfect candidate for this role because..." (too aggressive)
- ❌ "Can you refer me?" (immediate ask)
- ❌ No message at all (60% of requests have no note—don't be one of them)

### 5. Follow-Up After Connection

**Within 48 hours of acceptance:**
- Send a brief thank-you message
- Reiterate your interest in the company
- Offer value: share a relevant article, insight, or ask a thoughtful question about their work
- If appropriate, mention you've applied and would welcome the chance to discuss the role

**Example:**
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]! I've been following [Company]'s growth in [space] and am really impressed by [specific initiative]. I applied for the AE role and would love to chat if there's a fit. Either way, excited to stay connected.

**If no immediate role:**
- Stay engaged: comment on their posts, share relevant content
- Check in every 4-6 weeks with something of value (not just "any openings?")
- Build a genuine relationship—most hires come from warm networks, not cold applications

## Best Practices

### Optimize Your Profile First

Before reaching out, ensure your LinkedIn profile is recruiter-ready:
- **Headline:** Results-focused with metrics (e.g., "SaaS Account Executive | 150% Quota Attainment | Enterprise Sales")
- **About section:** Clear value prop, key achievements, target role
- **Experience:** Quantified results (revenue, quota %, deal sizes, retention rates)
- **Skills:** Match job descriptions (e.g., Salesforce, consultative selling, pipeline management)
- **Open to Work:** Use "Recruiters only" setting if currently employed

### Manage Volume Wisely

- **Connection limit:** Max 20-25 requests per day to avoid LinkedIn flags
- **Quality over quantity:** 5 highly personalized requests > 25 generic ones
- **Track outreach:** Spreadsheet or tool to log who you contacted, when, and response status

### Avoid Common Mistakes

- **Don't blast generic messages:** Personalization increases acceptance by 3x
- **Don't ignore company instructions:** If a job post says "do not contact recruiters," respect it
- **Don't be pushy:** Let the relationship develop; most AE hires take 4-8 weeks from first contact
- **Don't forget to apply:** Networking complements applications, it doesn't replace them

### Combine with Other Channels

- **Job boards:** LinkedIn is one channel—also use Built In, AngelList, company career pages
- **Referrals:** 40% higher interview rate—ask your network for intros
- **Direct outreach:** Email hiring managers if you can find their contact info (use tools like Hunter.io)

## Why This Works

- **Referrals bypass filters:** Warm connections move you past ATS screening
- **Visibility matters:** 80% of jobs are filled through networking, not applications
- **Timing is key:** Early applicants (first 24-48 hours) get 10x more attention
- **Relationships compound:** Today's "not now" can be next quarter's "we're hiring"

## Measuring Success

- **Acceptance rate:** Aim for 40-50% connection acceptance
- **Response rate:** 15-25% of accepted connections should reply
- **Interview rate:** 5-10% of connections should lead to conversations
- **Adjust messaging:** If acceptance is low, increase personalization; if response is low, refine your follow-up

## Key Mindset

Approach this as relationship-building, not transactional job-hunting. You're creating a network that will serve you for years, not just this search. Be genuine, offer value, and play the long game. Most AE roles are filled through relationships, not job boards—this skill helps you build those relationships strategically.
