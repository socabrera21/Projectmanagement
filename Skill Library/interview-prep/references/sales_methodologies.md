# Sales Methodologies Reference

## MEDDPICC Framework

### M - Metrics
Quantifiable measures of customer success and business impact
- What specific, measurable results will the solution deliver?
- How will success be measured?
- What are the before/after metrics?

**Interview Application:**
- Reference specific metrics from past deals (time saved, cost reduced, revenue increased)
- Quantify your own performance (quota attainment, deal sizes, win rates)

### E - Economic Buyer
The person with budget authority and final decision-making power
- Who controls the budget?
- Who can say "yes" when everyone else says "no"?

**Interview Application:**
- Demonstrate experience identifying and engaging economic buyers
- Show examples of navigating to C-suite stakeholders
- Explain how you've aligned solutions to executive priorities

### D - Decision Criteria
The formal and informal factors used to evaluate solutions
- What are the must-haves vs. nice-to-haves?
- How will they make the final decision?
- What's the evaluation process?

**Interview Application:**
- Show how you've uncovered and influenced decision criteria
- Demonstrate consultative discovery skills
- Explain competitive differentiation strategies

### D - Decision Process
The steps, timeline, and stakeholders involved in making a decision
- What's the timeline?
- Who needs to approve?
- What are the steps from evaluation to signature?

**Interview Application:**
- Demonstrate deal management and forecasting accuracy
- Show ability to navigate complex procurement processes
- Explain stakeholder mapping and influence strategies

### P - Paper Process
Legal, procurement, and contracting requirements
- What's the procurement process?
- Are there legal or security reviews?
- What's the contracting timeline?

**Interview Application:**
- Show experience with enterprise contracts and negotiations
- Demonstrate collaboration with legal and procurement teams
- Explain how you've accelerated or managed contract processes

### I - Identify Pain
The critical business problems the customer needs to solve
- What's not working today?
- What's the cost of doing nothing?
- Why now?

**Interview Application:**
- Demonstrate discovery and questioning skills
- Show ability to uncover and articulate pain points
- Explain how you've created urgency

### C - Champion
An internal advocate who sells on your behalf
- Who will sell for you when you're not in the room?
- Do they have influence and credibility?
- Are they personally invested in the project's success?

**Interview Application:**
- Show examples of building champions
- Demonstrate relationship-building skills
- Explain multi-threading strategies

### C - Competition
Alternative solutions the customer is considering
- What other solutions are they evaluating?
- What's the status quo/do-nothing option?
- How do we differentiate?

**Interview Application:**
- Demonstrate competitive intelligence gathering
- Show strategic positioning and differentiation
- Explain how you've won competitive deals

## Value Selling

### Core Principles
1. Focus on business outcomes, not product features
2. Quantify the value in customer terms
3. Connect solution to strategic initiatives
4. Build consensus around value realization

### Value Selling Process
1. **Discovery**: Uncover pain points and business objectives
2. **Value Hypothesis**: Develop initial ROI/value case
3. **Validation**: Test and refine with stakeholders
4. **Consensus**: Build agreement around value
5. **Commitment**: Align on success metrics and timeline

**Interview Application:**
- Provide examples of ROI/business case development
- Show consultative approach focused on outcomes
- Demonstrate ability to speak to business value, not just features

## Prospecting Best Practices

### Research-Driven Prospecting
- Company news and triggers (funding, expansion, leadership changes)
- Industry trends and challenges
- Personal connection points (shared background, interests)

### Multi-Channel Approach
- Email, phone, LinkedIn, events
- Sequenced outreach with value at each touchpoint
- Personalization at scale

### Messaging Framework
- Hook: Relevant insight or trigger
- Relevance: Why this matters to them specifically
- Value: What you can help them achieve
- Call to Action: Clear next step

**Interview Application:**
- Share specific prospecting metrics and conversion rates
- Demonstrate creativity and persistence
- Show systematic approach to pipeline generation

## Team Selling

### Cross-Functional Collaboration

- Customer Success: Implementation and adoption planning

- Leadership: Executive alignment and escalation

### Orchestrating Resources
- When to bring in which resources
- Preparing teammates for customer interactions
- Post-meeting debriefs and next steps

**Interview Application:**
- Provide examples of complex team selling situations
- Show leadership in coordinating resources
- Demonstrate collaboration and influence skills

## Driving Urgency

### Creating Urgency
- Business impact of delay
- Limited-time opportunities (budget cycles, promotions)
- Risk of competitive loss
- Strategic initiative timelines

### Urgency vs. Pressure
- Urgency is based on customer's business needs
- Pressure is based on seller's needs
- Authentic urgency drives better outcomes

**Interview Application:**
- Show examples of creating authentic urgency
- Demonstrate understanding of buyer timing
- Explain how you've accelerated deals without being pushy

## Objection Handling

### Common Objections
- **Price**: Focus on value and ROI, not cost
- **Timing**: Understand real timing vs. put-off
- **Competition**: Differentiate on unique value
- **Status Quo**: Articulate cost of inaction

### Framework
1. Listen and validate
2. Clarify and isolate
3. Respond with value
4. Confirm resolution

**Interview Application:**
- Share specific objection handling examples
- Demonstrate resilience and adaptability
- Show consultative approach to concerns
